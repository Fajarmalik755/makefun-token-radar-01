Method. Snapshot: DefiLlama api.llama.fi/protocols, 15 Nov 2026 (9,050 protocols), reconciled at build time against the competition's sealed snapshot of 14 Nov 2026 so the board covers every protocol on its 40-protocol watchlist. A protocol counts as tokenless when neither it nor any sibling in its DefiLlama parent family records a CoinGecko id, CoinMarketCap id or market cap, and no CoinGecko coin matches its symbol + name, exact name, or clean single-word name. Bridges, liquid staking / restaking, wrapped, staked and bridged products, CEXs and chains are excluded. Three manual overrides are documented in scripts/build_board.py. Full method in the README.

Implied value is an estimate, not a prediction — many of these protocols may never launch a token. Not financial advice. This page makes no network requests: all data is bundled in data.js, written beside this page by node main.ts, and it works fully offline.
