How this board was built

The formula

expected = TVL × ratiocat × size × growth × maturity × odds

Implied value starts from the traded comps: log-log OLS log(mcap) = —, fitted on — traded tokens that record both a market cap and a TVL. The category ratio is the median mcap/TVL of that category's comps, shrunk toward the global median (8 pseudo-counts) and capped at — (2× the global upper quartile). Size corrects with the log-log slope, so bigger protocols earn a lower multiple. Growth is the 7-day change clamped to −60…+120%, halved into a multiplier. Maturity rises 0.6 → 1.0 over three years since listing. Token odds are the traded share of the protocol's own age / TVL / growth bucket, shrunk toward the global traded rate (—). Rank is by expected value, highest first.

Held-out validation — the fit tested, not just fitted

Split-half is run twice (even/odd by slug, then first/second half), fitting on one half and testing on the other, both directions, so every comp is scored held-out. Leave-one-out refits on the other 127 comps for each comp in turn.

Limits — read before trusting a number

  • The fit is honest but weak: R² = —. TVL alone explains little of market cap; the interval (low–high) on each row is wide for that reason.
  • Snapshot is sealed at —. Nothing here is live; prices and TVL have moved since.
  • “Pre-token” means the snapshot records no token for the protocol — not proof it has no token.
  • Category ratios are capped at 2× the global upper quartile; a one-comp category cannot set the board.
  • Farm maths assumes one qualifying wallet takes 1/20,000 of the implied value. Verdicts need a margin of safety: FARM at 10× cost, THIN at 3×, else SKIP.
  • Gas is a snapshot-date estimate per chain; real gas, eligibility rules and airdrop criteria are the protocol's own, and may exclude farmers entirely.
